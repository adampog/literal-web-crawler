# Arch Linux artwork source

The optional abdomen mark is selected explicitly with marking: arch. The
hourglass remains the default. Arch Linux does not endorse this plugin.

## Arch Linux

`archMarkPath` transcribes all three paths from
`/usr/share/pixmaps/archlinux-logo.svg`, including the small TM paths. Its cubic
curves and proportions are retained; it is rotated toward the head and rendered
with the requested red, translucent fill and glow. At desktop size the TM detail
is below a pixel and cannot be read. The file belongs to installed package
`filesystem 2025.10.12-1`; that package's installed copyright/permission notice
is copied to `licenses/arch-filesystem.txt`. The SVG itself has no licence metadata;
the package notice does not settle separate logo trademark permission.

Official artwork: https://archlinux.org/art/ links the logo to the trademark
policy: https://terms.archlinux.org/docs/trademark-policy/ (checked 2026-10-08).
The policy permits specified noncommercial community uses without implying
endorsement; other uses require permission. It requires the TM declaration to
remain intact and scaling to preserve proportions, and discusses monochrome
versions on busy backgrounds. A red outlined translucent mascot treatment is
not expressly addressed. Keeping its TM paths in this optional marking does not
establish permission to distribute that treatment. Before a release chooses this
variant, the human should resolve that use against the policy, including the
unreadable TM detail. No trademark permission has been requested or obtained.
